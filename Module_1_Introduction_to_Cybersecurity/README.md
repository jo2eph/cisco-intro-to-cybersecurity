## 1.1 The World of Cybersecurity

### What is Cybersecurity?

- **Cybersecurity:** Ongoing effort to protect individuals, organizations, and governments from digital attacks by protecting networked systems and data from unauthorized use or harm

Three levels of protection:

- Personal
  - Your identity, data, and devices
- Organizational
  - Everyone's responsibility to protect organization's reputation, data, and customers
- Government
  - National security, economic stability, and safety and wellbeing of citizens

### Protecting Your Personal Data

**Personal data:** any information used to identify you, can exist both online and offline

Offline Identity:

- Real life persona
- Name
- Age
- Address
- Important NOT to overlook importance of securing offline data

Online Identity:

- Not just name
- Who you are and how you present yourself online
- Username or alias
- Social identity or online communities
- Limit the amount of personal information you reveal through online identity

### Your Data

How hackers can get their hands on your data:

- Medical records
  - Personal info about your health is added to your electronic health records (EHRs) each time you visit a doctor
  - Majority of these are saved online
  - Be aware of the medical info you share
- Education records
  - Info about academic qualifications and achievements
  - May also include contact info, disciplinary reports, health, etc
- Employment and financial records
  - Employment data can be valuable to hackers
  - Financial records may include info about income and expenditure
  - May include paychecks, credit card statements, credit rating, etc
  - All of this data can compromise privacy
